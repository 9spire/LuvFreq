Luv Freq is named for 528 Hz - the love frequency - heart-centered sound for connection and transformation.

On my 45th birthday in 2022, I decided to learn how to produce electronic music. The goal was simple and ambitious: finish an entire album by the end of the year. I bought the best laptop I could afford, a copy of Logic Pro, and dug in.

Innumerable YouTube videos and endless hours later, 22 45 was born - named for the year and the age. It's a reflection of that exercise: an exploration of song structure, playful sounds, experimentation recording voice and instruments, and a deep dive on preparing music for mastering and release.

Based in Portland, Oregon. For DJ mixes and booking, visit Ms Match.
